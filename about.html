About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2015 in a small rented kitchen, with one oven and a sourdough starter passed down from our founder's grandmother. Neighbors began stopping by on their way to work, and word spread. Today we bake for hundreds of local families, commuters, and community events each week, but every loaf is still shaped by hand.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good bread starts with good ingredients and good relationships. That is why we:

  • Buy flour from a regional mill that works with family farms
  • Use eggs, butter, and dairy from nearby producers
  • Build our seasonal menu around fruit from local orchards
  • Donate unsold bread to a neighborhood food pantry each evening
